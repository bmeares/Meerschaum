## Notes

- Wave 9 (task Q): `_core.get_config` read-only fast path (PARITY row 12) and `Pipe.verify` routed to
  Rust (`_core.verify_pipe`). Against `2026-10-13-w8-perf.md` on the same host, the façade (`v5py`)
  improved on every sync case: `sync_100k` 5.84 s -> 4.80 s, `sync_1M` 54.3 s -> 42.0 s,
  `inc_100k` 5.36 s -> 4.44 s, `inc_1M` 5.60 s -> 4.52 s (sync cases are not affected by the verify
  routing; most of the gain should be the `get_config` fast path, but other wave-9 changes are in the same tree). The `v5cli` column is a rebuilt release `mrsm`.
